ABC Technologies - Pricing Guide
===============================

1. Subscription Plans
----------------------
ABC Technologies offers three subscription plans tailored to different business sizes:

* Basic Plan
  - Price: $29 per user/month (billed monthly) or $299 per user/year (billed annually - saves ~15%).
  - Target: Small teams and startups.
  - Features:
    * Access to core business management modules (contacts, task tracking, simple invoicing).
    * 10 GB secure cloud storage.
    * Support: Standard email support (response time within 24-48 hours).
    * Limit: Maximum of 5 users per account.

* Professional Plan (Most Popular)
  - Price: $79 per user/month (billed monthly) or $799 per user/year (billed annually - saves ~15%).
  - Target: Growing businesses and mid-sized enterprises.
  - Features:
    * All Basic Plan features.
    * Advanced CRM integrations, project management boards, automated invoicing, and expenses.
    * 100 GB secure cloud storage.
    * Support: Priority email and live chat support (response time within 4-12 hours).
    * Limit: Maximum of 50 users per account.

* Enterprise Plan
  - Price: Custom pricing (starts at $199 per user/month, billed annually). Contact Sales for a custom quote.
  - Target: Large organizations requiring advanced security, custom integrations, and dedicated support.
  - Features:
    * All Professional Plan features.
    * Custom database integrations, single sign-on (SSO), advanced audit logs, and API access.
    * Unlimited cloud storage.
    * Support: 24/7 dedicated support (response time within 1 hour) with a dedicated account manager.
    * SLA: 99.9% uptime guarantee with credit eligibility.
    * Limit: Unlimited users.

2. Billing and Payment Methods
-----------------------------
- Accepted Payment Methods: We accept major Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and PayPal for all plans.
- Wire Transfers: Direct bank wire transfers are available for Enterprise Plan annual subscriptions only.
- Invoicing: Invoices are automatically generated and emailed on the first day of each billing cycle (monthly or annually). Invoices can also be downloaded from the User Dashboard.
